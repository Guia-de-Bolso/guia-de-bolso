# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (v2.1).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the product

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to **contato@guiadebolso.app**. The maintainer will review and respond as appropriate. All complaints will be kept confidential.

For a full text of the covenant, see [contributor-covenant.org](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
